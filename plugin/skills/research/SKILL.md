# Research

Use MarketMirror's `research` MCP tool with a concrete query, market and relevant sources. Record the source URL, date, market and quality for every finding. Check blocked sources in the returned status; never replace missing data with invented quotes. Use `understand` to turn product material into a profile. Pass the resulting profile and cited findings to `campaign_plan`.

Public comments describe people who chose to post; they are not a representative customer sample. Keep conflicting evidence and unknowns visible.
