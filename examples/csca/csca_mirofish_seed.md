# CSCA.ai Meta Ads — Simulation Seed Document

## 1. Product

CSCA.ai is an independent exam-prep platform for the CSCA (China Scholastic Competency Assessment), the academic test international students take when applying for undergraduate admission and scholarships at Chinese universities. It is built by WiseAdmit, a study-in-China admissions company. CSCA.ai is not affiliated with the official exam body.

What it offers:
- 2,400+ practice questions in Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry
- 30 full-length mock tests, timed like the real exam
- Instant step-by-step feedback on every answer
- Live classes with teachers covering the Maths, Physics and Chemistry syllabus
- Free practice questions (no account needed for the first questions); a free mock test after signing up
- Guides on registration, subject formats and syllabus

Main calls to action on the site: "Start Free Practice", "Start Free Test".

## 2. Audiences

Countries: Nepal, Bangladesh, Indonesia, Vietnam, Malaysia, Sri Lanka.

Segment A — Students (17–22): finishing high school (+2 / A-levels / SMA), considering study abroad, price-sensitive, mostly on mobile and often on slower mobile data, very active on Facebook and Instagram. Many have heard of Chinese government scholarships but don't know what CSCA is or whether they need it. Concerns: "Am I good enough?", "Is this a scam?", "How hard is the test?", "Is it really free?"

Segment B — Parents (40–55): pay for and approve the study-abroad decision. Mostly on Facebook. Concerns: total cost, safety of studying in China, whether the degree is recognised, trustworthiness of agents, and whether the scholarship is real.

Segment C — Broad audience (Meta picks the targeting).

Objective: Landing page views (not sign-ups).

"Want to study in China with a scholarship? Many students know about Chinese universities, but they are not always sure how to check if they are ready to apply. That's where CSCA comes in. CSCA helps show your academic readiness for Chinese university admission and scholarship opportunities. Think of it as an important first step before starting your China study journey. Take the Free Mock CSCA Test today and see where you stand."

Creative 2 — headline "Your China Study Path", CTA "Learn More":
"Still deciding where to study abroad? China could be a smart option for students looking for international exposure, strong universities, and scholarship opportunities. The Free Mock CSCA Test helps you understand your current readiness before you begin the full application process. Check your level today and plan your next step with confidence."

Results (lifetime):
- Spend $56.06; 275,000 impressions; 3,791 link clicks; 1,500 landing page views; 16 registrations
- Only 40% of clicks became a landing page view (slow mobile page, or accidental clicks)
- About 1.1% of landing page visitors registered; about $3.50 per registration
- Instagram click rate 2.62% vs Facebook 1.41%
- Cheapest: Nepal and Bangladesh (~$0.03 per landing page view). Highest click rate: Indonesia (1.9–2.3%)

## 4. New ad concepts to test

See the companion file "csca_new_ads.md". The ten concepts (numbers match that file):
1. Free score check ("See your CSCA score free")
2. Scholarship eligibility ("Could you qualify for a Chinese scholarship?")
3. "What is CSCA?" explainer (education first)
4. Sample-question challenge ("Can you solve this CSCA maths question?")
5. Parents: cost and planning
6. Parents: trust and readiness
7. Retargeting: "You started, finish your free test"
8. Reel: 15-second screen recording of the mock test
9. Weak-topic diagnosis ("Find your weak topics in Physics")
10. Country-specific (Nepal / Bangladesh / Indonesia)
